# RuneQuest Weapons & Equipment - Research Notes

Source: *RuneQuest: Weapons & Equipment* (Chaosium, 2023), 124 pages.
Read: pages 1-114 (pages 115-124 are Training rules and system appendices - skipped as out of scope).

This document records what the book contains and what WSR can borrow, inspire from, or skip.
It is a research input, not a decision log. Decisions go in `decisions.md`.

---

## Summary

RuneQuest W&E is a standalone equipment supplement for RuneQuest Glorantha (RQG), itself a BRP derivative.
It is a bronze-age fantasy world book, closely aligned with WSR's ancient-world setting rubric (design.md §1.2 #3:
Mediterranean, Levantine, Mesopotamian, Persian, Steppe; bronze and iron age). The setting assumptions are
genre-adjacent but not identical: Glorantha has divine magic baked into the economy, non-human species, and
world-specific coinage. All borrowing requires translation, not direct lift.

The book fills every gap identified in `notes/source-13-equipment.md`:
- Mundane gear: extensive (clothing, tools, food, light, containers, rope, writing supplies, instruments)
- Currency: full tiered coinage system with market-size rules
- Mounts and beasts of burden: complete (price, carrying capacity, skills)
- Vehicles: carts, wagons, palanquins, chariots with stats
- Watercraft: full table (rafts through triremes) with structure points and crew
- Weapons: several types BRP UGE lacks or treats sparsely
- Armor: per-location tables with AP values across many types
- Special rules: material tiers (copper/bronze/iron), ENC system, masterwork items, weapon types

Currency system uses Lunars (L), Clacks (C = 1/20 L), Wheels (W = 20 L), Bolgs (B = 1/4 C).
All prices in this document are given in Lunars unless otherwise noted.

## Priority 2: Special Weapon and Armor Rules

These are RQ W&E mechanical rules worth considering for WSR adaptation.

### Material Tiers

Three primary metals in bronze-age fantasy:
- **Copper/Tin (primitive):** AP/HP half of bronze. Weapons at -1 damage die step. Cheapest.
- **Bronze (standard):** Baseline stats. The default for all WSR pricing and AP values.
- **Iron (superior):** HP is half again bronze (x1.5 round up). Enchanted iron: AP is half again bronze. Unenchanted iron imposes -5% to all sorcery skills per ENC of iron carried.

Iron penalty to sorcery is a WSR-relevant rule. It creates a built-in tension between the fully armored iron warrior and the sorcerer who must limit iron exposure. This fits design.md §2.2 ("magic is alien, costly, and costly in indirect ways").

Iron pricing: iron weapons typically cost 5-10x bronze equivalents; iron armor 10-20x.

### Masterwork Items

Masterwork weapons and armor cost 5-10x standard and require a skilled crafter. Effects:
- Masterwork weapon: +5% to skill or +1 damage (crafter chooses at build time)
- Masterwork armor: +1 AP or -1 ENC (crafter chooses)
- Masterwork items also confer social status and can add to Reputation in appropriate settings

This is a clean, lightweight system. Worth borrowing for WSR's GM Book as an optional layer.

### Weapon Type Special Effects

RQ W&E classifies weapons by damage type and assigns different critical/fumble effects:
- **Crushing (C):** Criticals may knock unconscious (CON roll) or break bones
- **Impaling (I):** Criticals cause weapon to lodge in wound (additional damage on removal)
- **Slashing (S):** Criticals cause bleeding wounds (ongoing damage per round)
- **Cut-and-thrust (CT):** Can choose between impaling and slashing on a critical

BRP UGE already has its own special success effects. Cross-check during chapter drafting - RQ's system may provide refinements worth borrowing for specific weapon categories.

### Reach and Combat Spacing

RQ W&E notes weapon reach categories (Close, Short, Medium, Long, Very Long) that interact with
combat positioning. BRP UGE has reach built into the weapon table. This is already covered in BRP UGE
but RQ W&E's framing may offer tighter language for WSR's chapter.

### Armor Repair

Rules for field-repairing damaged armor:
- Leather: repaired with Craft (Leather), tools, and time
- Metal: requires Craft (Armourer), forge access, and time proportional to damage
- Full restoration requires a smithy
- Field repairs restore partial AP/HP at GM discretion

This is worth a brief note in the WSR equipment chapter - no separate subsystem needed, just guidance.

### Encumbrance (ENC)

RQ W&E uses a fine-grained ENC system where fractional encumbrance items (shown in parentheses as
decimal values) accumulate. Items under (0.5) ENC individually are counted in groups of 10 as 1 ENC.
BRP UGE has its own ENC system. The RQ approach of fractional ENC for small items is more granular
and may conflict with BRP UGE defaults. Note for chapter drafting: don't import the ENC values
directly - verify against BRP UGE's encumbrance rules first.

### Coin Tiers

| Coin | Abbreviation | Equivalent | Notes |

| Bolg | B | 1/20 Clack | Very small; copper chip |
| Clack | C | 1/20 Lunar | Copper; everyday transactions |
| Lunar | L | 1 Lunar | Silver; standard unit |
| Wheel | W | 20 Lunars | Gold; large transactions, land |

For WSR, the Gloranthan names are setting-specific and should be replaced with generic ancient-world coin names or left to the GM Book to define. Suggested mapping:
- **Bolg** -> copper chip / obol
- **Clack** -> copper coin / as / obol (varies by culture)
- **Lunar** -> silver coin / denarius / shekel
- **Wheel** -> gold coin / stater / gold talent (fraction)

A laborer earns approximately 1-2 L per week. A mercenary soldier earns 5-10 L per week.
A good sword costs 20-50 L. A horse costs 50-150 L. A small house costs 2-15 L (building materials).

### Market Sizes

RQ W&E defines market size affecting availability:
- **Hamlet:** Basic goods only; no specialist items
- **Village:** Common goods; simple weapons and armor
- **Town:** Most goods; common adventuring equipment
- **City:** All standard goods; rare items possible
- **Major City:** Exotic items, unusual materials, masterwork available

Availability by market size is a useful GM tool. Worth adapting for WSR's GM Book.

## Borrow / Inspire / Skip Recommendations

### Borrow Directly (adapt for WSR)

1. **Mundane gear tables** - the full common goods lists (clothing, tools, containers, light, rope, food) should be the primary source for WSR's equipment chapter. Strip Gloranthan names where they appear, keep the items and price brackets.

2. **Currency tier structure** - the four-coin hierarchy (copper chip / copper coin / silver coin / gold coin) maps cleanly to ancient-world economies and gives WSR a calibrated price ladder. Replace Gloranthan coin names.

3. **Material tier rules (copper/bronze/iron)** - directly applicable. The iron-versus-sorcery penalty deserves serious consideration as a WSR-specific rule (design.md §2.2 fit).

4. **Market size availability** - hamlet/village/town/city tiers for what can be purchased. Clean GM guidance layer.

5. **Beast of burden and mount table** - prices, carrying capacity, and riding gear are directly usable with minor translation.

6. **Chariot stats** - the chariot table is a strong inclusion for WSR's ancient-world register, either in the Player Book or GM Book.

7. **Boat passage prices and travel rates** - river and coastal sea travel is a major genre element (Conan crosses seas repeatedly). These rates anchor the fiction.

### Inspire (use as design input, don't lift directly)

1. **Armor per-location AP table** - WSR uses Major Wounds (no per-location HP), so the location-by-location table doesn't port directly. Use the chest AP values as the armor protection value; adapt the helmet table separately.

2. **Masterwork item rules** - the 5-10x cost / +5% or +1 damage / -1 ENC system is elegant. WSR should have a simpler version (perhaps just "superior quality: +1 AP or +1 damage, costs 3x") that doesn't require tracking who made what.

3. **Weapon special effects by damage type** - slashing/impaling/crushing criticals already exist in BRP UGE. RQ W&E's version may offer refinements; cross-check during chapter drafting rather than importing wholesale.

4. **Hireling rates** - useful as GM calibration but should be adapted to match WSR's coin system once that is designed.

### Skip (Glorantha-specific or out of scope)

1. **Dwellings / land ownership / Sacred Time** - deeply embedded in Gloranthan clan economy. Not applicable to WSR without major reworking. Low value.

## Watch-Items

1. **ENC compatibility:** RQ W&E's fractional ENC system (items listed as 0.1, 0.2, etc.) may not map cleanly to BRP UGE's encumbrance scale. Verify BRP UGE's ENC rules before adopting any weight values from RQ W&E. May need to convert or round.

2. **Iron rules require a decision before the chapter is drafted:** The iron-vs-sorcery penalty is a meaningful rules choice. If adopted, it becomes part of the sorcery chapter (cost architecture) as well as the equipment chapter. Do not draft equipment without resolving this.

3. **Composite bow distinctness:** BRP UGE may already have composite bow. Verify the source PDF before adding it as a "gap." If it's there, no addition needed; if it's there but underspecced, a note in the chapter text may be sufficient.

4. **Chariot placement:** Chariots straddle the Player Book / GM Book boundary. They are equipment (Player Book) but primarily a battlefield element and encounter prop (GM Book). Decide placement before drafting.

5. **Stirrups:** Whether to include stirrups as an item depends on the setting's technological floor. They are historically post-Roman in widespread use. If WSR's world is strictly pre-stirrup, omit the item. If the setting is flexible (Moorcock's eternal champion multiverse suggests flexibility), include them as a market-dependent item.

6. **Coin system design:** WSR needs its own coin names before the equipment chapter can be finalized. This is a design.md / GM Book question, not a source-mining question. Flag as an open design decision.

## Open Questions to Escalate

1. **What are WSR's coin names and denominations?** The currency chapter cannot be drafted without this. RuneQuest's four-tier system (copper chip / copper / silver / gold) is the recommended structure, but the names are a design decision for the setting tone.

2. **Does WSR adopt the iron-sorcery penalty?** This affects both the equipment chapter and the sorcery chapter. The rule is genre-resonant and fits the cost architecture locked in `decisions.md`, but it needs an explicit decision entry.

3. **Does WSR have a stand-alone mundane gear chapter, or is equipment folded into the Player Book as a section?** The answer affects how much detail the list needs and whether prices are presented as tables or as GM guidance ranges.

4. **Is the masterwork item system a Player Book rule (players can seek masterwork items) or a GM Book tool (GMs price special items at their discretion)?** The answer affects how explicitly it needs to be specified.

5. **Should WSR provide a simplified chariot/vehicle section?** Genre strongly supports it (Conan, the various Howard stories with chariots, Moorcock's eternal champion battles). A one-page vehicle rules section in the GM Book would cover chariots, wagons, and river craft.
