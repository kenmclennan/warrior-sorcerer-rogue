### Phase 1 decisions locked (in `decisions.md`)

11 design decisions locked, including:

- **Magic dropped** - one magic system: Sorcery (no secondary "hedge magic" tier)
- **Sorcery cost architecture:** PP (immediate, shared with Fate Points), Patron Score (relational, optional via canonised Allegiance mechanic), Corruption (permanent, sorcerer-only via canonised Sanity mechanic)
- **Combat resolution:** matching-dice criticals within success range + weapon-type crit tables (Rolemaster-inspired prose); matching-dice fumbles (Delta Green-inspired); PP-buy mechanic to convert success to critical
- **Major Wounds default; no per-location HP** even as optional
- **Mook rule:** Pulp Cthulhu's "incapacitated at half HP" + "Look Out Master!"
- **Talent system:** Pulp Cthulhu's structural model (4 tables, choose 2 at chargen, gate PP spends) - WSR-original talent list
- **Equipment:** extensive borrows from RuneQuest W&E for mundane gear, currency, additional weapons/armour, mounts/vehicles
- **Iron-vs-sorcery rule:** -5% sorcery per ENC of unenchanted iron carried
- **Borrowing principle:** non-ORC sources contribute mechanisms only; rules text and tables are WSR-original
- **G8 Sample Material chapter dropped** from GM Book; sample adventures deferred to a separate post-core supplement
